Our SEO Methodology

Unlike basic agencies, we look under the hood. Our holistic SEO approach covers technical foundations, content authority, and powerful off-page signals.

1. Technical SEO & Architecture

If search engines can't crawl your site efficiently, no amount of content will save your rankings. We conduct exhaustive technical audits to fix crawl errors, optimize site speed, and establish a flawless site architecture.

  • Core Web Vitals Optimization

    Ensuring LCP, FID, and CLS scores pass Google's strictest thresholds.

  • Schema Markup & Structured Data

    Injecting JSON-LD schema to win rich snippets in search results.

2. Content & Topical Authority

Google rewards expertise. We craft comprehensive, high-quality content strategies designed to establish your brand as the undisputed authority in your niche through topic clusters and pillar pages.

  • Deep Keyword Research

    Finding high-volume, low-competition keywords with strong buyer intent.

  • Pillar Page Creation

    Writing 3,000+ word definitive guides that naturally attract backlinks.

3. High-Tier Link Building & Digital PR

Backlinks remain one of Google's strongest ranking signals. We acquire highly relevant, powerful backlinks from authoritative publications within your industry through genuine outreach and digital PR.

  • Editorial Outreach

    Securing placements on DR 70+ websites.

  • Competitor Gap Analysis

    Stealing your competitors' best backlinks through superior content.

Execution Process

1

Discovery & Audit

We begin by tearing down your current website. We analyze your Google Analytics, Search Console, and backlink profile. We identify every single technical error, crawl anomaly, and lost ranking opportunity.

2

Keyword Mapping & Strategy

We map out a comprehensive keyword architecture. We assign specific, high-intent keywords to every single page of your site, ensuring zero keyword cannibalization and maximum relevance for search intent.

3

On-Page & Technical Execution

Our developers and SEOs work in tandem to fix all site speed issues, implement proper schema, and optimize all meta tags, headers, and internal linking structures across the entire domain.

4

Ongoing Growth & Reporting

SEO is a marathon. Every month, we execute our content and outreach plans. You receive transparent, detailed reports showing exact ranking movements, traffic increases, and leads generated.
